Which sheet, when?

  1. Spell Library phase — everyone fills a Spellsheet for the book they brought, in secret, then places that book in the center.
  2. Character Creation phase — steal a different book from the center and fill a Thief Sheet from it.
  3. You cast from the Tome you stole — using the spellsheet someone else wrote, whose effects you won't know until they fire.
