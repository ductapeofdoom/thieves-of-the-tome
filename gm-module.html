GM Module

Embody Na'zar — Build the Tomeality

👀 For the Game Master. Build this from your book while the players write spells and make thieves. Introduce the Tomeality and Escape Condition aloud; keep the rest as your reference.
Working locally

1 The Tomeality

The setting, drawn from your book. Answer four questions.

Overt: everyone knows magic is real (often fantasy). Covert: the White Guard hide it (often cyberpunk).

Examples: The royal family of England seeking to live forever. A megacorp fixated on profit margins and commodifying magic. A secretive sect aiming to control world affairs. An artisan guild that only accepts the best of the best.
Examples: A hollowed-out mushroom rife with rabid worshipers. A mech palace that cannot be reached from the ground. A moonbase only accessible by rocket.

2 Escape Condition

One sentence describing what the thieves must do to escape, built from the last pages of your book. Aim for at least 3 explicit steps; don't make any single step too hard.

3 The Palette

Flip to random pages and jot 3–4 entries per column. You'll discover their meaning as you play.

Locations

Places a scene might happen.

Obstacles

Object/situation → a mostly negative effect.

Actions

A verb → an effect an NPC can inflict.

Golden rule when stuck: the thieves' goal is at a location, but an obstacle stands in the way, and an antagonist is using an action to stop them.

4 Antagonists

1–3 servants of the White Guard, each with a description and at least one magical power. (Robes can read magic, never exhaust spells, and memorize them.)
