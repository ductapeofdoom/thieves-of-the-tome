Thief of the Tome

Character Sheet — Library of Na'zar

1–2 Identity

Your name is the author of the Tome you stole (not the one you wrote spells for). Nicknames welcome.

3 Adjectives

Three mostly-positive descriptors from the first two pages of your Tome. Each grants +1 bonus die on relevant actions & reactions.

4 Inventory

Up to three backpack-sized objects from the first two pages. No inherent bonus, but situationally useful.

5 Destinies

One per random page (beginning / middle / end). Star when the "When I…" trigger happens in play — then "I will become…" grants bonus dice. Star at least one to escape.

6 Dooms

One negative descriptor per random page. Star when you fail a reaction — starred dooms subtract hazard dice. 4 starred dooms = you meet your doom.

7 The Heist

How did your thief contribute to stealing the Tomes? "Yes, and…" — no rolls. Your specialty (above) comes from this narration.
